# Changelog

All notable changes to ClassicUI Forever: Standalone Nameplates are documented here. It has version numbers of its own, apart from the full ClassicUI Forever's.

## [1.0.3] - 2026-11-04

### Changed
- On Forever, a chat line at login thanks you for playing on the new client and links the page to report bugs, so they get fixed as soon as possible.

## [1.0.2] - 2026-11-03

### Changed
- The size box in Default toggles' confirm names the game's UI Scale and the percent it sets.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-30

### Changed
- Changing target or focus lays out only the two nameplates involved, not every plate on screen.
- The addon's own nameplate art (borders, cast bar frames, the glow) is drawn once per plate, not again on every update and every cast.
- Player nameplates check whether their color needs to change once a second instead of five times, which counts most in cities and battlegrounds.
- A buff or debuff change on one nameplate updates the rings around that plate's aura icons only, not those on every plate.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-29

### Changed
- Version numbers of its own from now on, starting at 1.0.0. Earlier files carried the full addon's numbers (0.40.5).
- The settings window has a search box.

### Fixed
- The welcome note (right-click the minimap button) is about this addon; it described the full addon's action bars and bags.
