# Changelog

All notable changes to ClassicUI Forever: Standalone Action Bars are documented here. It has version numbers of its own, apart from the full ClassicUI Forever's.

## [1.1.2] - 2026-11-04

### Changed
- On Forever, a chat line at login thanks you for playing on the new client and links the page to report bugs, so they get fixed as soon as possible.

## [1.1.1] - 2026-11-03

### Changed
- The interface size questions and check boxes name the game's UI Scale and the percent they set, in fewer words. The classic layout's size question also says what your UI Scale is now.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-30

### Changed
- With Bag bar on backpack only, a click on the backpack shows the other bags or folds them away again, in place of the small arrow beside it. It is Ctrl-click by default; Show bags with picks Alt, Shift or a right-click instead.
- Hide key ring art also clears the key ring's own slot on the classic bar, not only its art when the key ring is moved off the bar. It takes over from the Hide key ring art too box under Hide bags bar art, which is gone; where that box was checked, Hide key ring art is checked for you.
- With Classic bar off, a Bag bar pick the game's own bag bar cannot follow is grayed, and its tooltip says why: the whole pick on Forever, where the game's bar keeps a socket for every bag, and Backpack only on retail, where the game's bar folds with its own arrow.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-29

### Changed
- Version numbers of its own from now on, starting at 1.0.0. Earlier files carried the full addon's numbers (0.40.3 and 0.40.5).
- The settings window has a search box.

### Fixed
- The welcome note (right-click the minimap button) is about this addon; it described the full addon's professions button and reagent bag.
