# Changelog

All notable changes to ClassicUI Forever: Standalone Cast Bars and Unit Frames are documented here. It has version numbers of its own, apart from the full ClassicUI Forever's.

## [1.1.4] - 2026-11-05

### Fixed
- With the game's own nameplates up and another addon drawing nameplate auras, new nameplates appearing no longer throw a Lua error from the theme (#237).

## [1.1.3] - 2026-11-04

### Fixed
- With Status Text on, the target and focus frames' health number on the right stays on the health bar; on Forever's launch build it stood past the portrait.

## [1.1.2] - 2026-11-04

### Changed
- On Forever, a chat line at login thanks you for playing on the new client and links the page to report bugs, so they get fixed as soon as possible.

## [1.1.1] - 2026-11-03

### Changed
- The size box in Default toggles' confirm names the game's UI Scale and the percent it sets.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-02

### Added
- Boss frames in the classic art: the boss frames of a dungeon or raid fight wear the old dragon frame, with the level in its round ring, the raid mark at the end of the bars and the threat percent over the name. Their cast bars sit clear of the art, beside the frame or under it as Edit Mode's Cast Bar on Side picks. On for everyone; uncheck Boss in the Frames list to get the game's own back.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-30

### Changed
- In a fight, the target and focus frames no longer put their aura rows back in place on every threat change.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-29

### Added
- First release: the 1.x player, target, focus, pet and party frames, cast bars and combo points from ClassicUI Forever, as an addon of their own.
