# ClassicUI Forever

Puts the original Classic look back on top of the modern interface in World of Warcraft: Forever. Every piece has its own toggle, so you can keep any of the new stuff you like.

## What it restores

### Bars and buttons

- The main bar with its gryphons and stone band, the micro menu and bags in their old sockets, and the old square buttons.
- Shaded XP and reputation bars, the key ring, and Classic-sized bag slots if you want them.

### Unit frames and combat

- Player, target, focus, target of target, pet and party frames with the old art, elite dragons and class colors.
- Classic cast bars, combo points, swing timers, the breath and fatigue bars, and classic damage numbers over the mob you hit.
- The 1.x nameplates, with the elite dragon and class colors if you like.
- The game's damage meter in the old look.

### Windows

- The 1.x spellbook, quest log and character sheet, with the old reputation, skills, PvP, currency and stats tabs.
- The classic talent window, with an option to show all three trees side by side.
- A classic Legacy window for Forever's Legacy trees, challenges and reward track, also with its three trees side by side if you like.
- The classic professions book, the 1.x trade skill window (or a wider double pane) and the classic trainer.
- The social window with the old friends list, Who list, guild roster and raid tabs, and the group finder dressed to match.
- Classic bags, or one bag for everything, with the reagent bag inside it if you like.
- The classic world map with its quest list, and Era's quest watch.
- The merchant, mail, quest, gossip, trade, bank and loot windows in the old metal border and stone, and the old loot roll frames.
- The game menu and settings as the old dialogs, with the old check boxes, sliders and drop downs.

### Minimap and chat

- The round minimap with the old tracking, mail, zoom, calendar and clock spots.
- Micro menu buttons as icons on the minimap ring, and a bag that collects other addons' minimap buttons.
- The old chat look.

### Themes

- Dark and Bronze themes for the classic art, and a flat color look.

## Move and size your windows

Unlock any window with the lock in its title bar, drag it anywhere, size it from its corner grip, and click reset to put it back. Reset windows in the options puts every window back at once. Windows open side by side as they did in Era.

An optional classic edit mode layout, offered on first login, puts the bars where 1.x kept them. Your current layout and keybinds are left alone.

## How it works

Every piece is a checkbox in the options window (`/fcui`) or in the game's Settings. Turn one off and the modern look comes back in place. The art is read from the game client, with bundled copies of the original Classic files as a fallback for the pieces the client no longer ships.

The addon also loads on retail, for the pieces retail has.

## Commands

- `/fcui` opens the options window; `/fcui settings` opens the same toggles in the game's Settings.
- `/fcui help` lists every toggle and command; `/fcui <toggle> on|off` sets one, for example `/fcui classicBar off`.
- `/fcui status` opens the status report: addon version, game build, the settings you changed and your other addons, to copy into a bug report.
- `/fcui layout` creates and selects the classic edit mode layout; `/fcui prompt` shows the first-login question again.
- `/fcui welcome` shows the welcome note again; `/fcui whatsnew` shows what changed in recent versions.
- `/fcui legacy` opens the classic Legacy window.
- `/fcui textures builtin|bundled` reads the art from the game client or from the copies in `media/`.
- `/fcui reset` puts every setting back to its default.
