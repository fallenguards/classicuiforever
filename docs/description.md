# ClassicUI Forever

[![GitHub](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fallenguards/EasyFind/main/.github/badges/github.png)](https://github.com/fallenguards/classicuiforever) [![Donate](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fallenguards/EasyFind/main/.github/badges/donate.png)](https://buymeacoffee.com/easyfind)

**ClassicUI Forever** puts the original Classic 1.x look back on the whole interface, on World of Warcraft: Forever and retail. Action bars, unit frames, nameplates, the minimap and world map, bags, chat, tooltips, menus and every game window, from the character sheet and spellbook to the merchant, mail and settings, look the way 1.x drew them. Every piece has its own toggle, so you can keep any of the modern parts you like, and a theme recolors it all in dark, bronze or any color you pick.

From the author of [EasyFind](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/easyfind), 4th place in the CurseForge 2026 World of Warcraft addon contest.

# How it works

Every piece is a checkbox in the options window (`/fcui`) or in the game's Settings. Turn one off and the modern look comes back in place. An edit mode layout, offered on first login, puts everything where 1.x kept it; your own layouts and keybinds are left alone. The art is read from the game client, with bundled copies of the original Classic files for the pieces the client no longer ships.

# Commands

*   `/fcui`: open the options window.
*   `/fcui help`: list every toggle and command.
*   `/fcui welcome`: show the welcome note again.
